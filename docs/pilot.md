# 18-Month Pilot

## Original Scope
The semester proposal asked for approval of an 18-month pilot across 25 hospitals and 500 clinics.

## Portfolio Refinement
Instructor feedback identified that the scope is ambitious and that the original proposal did not include cost figures.

### Months 0–3 — Setup
- select initial sites
- establish data-sharing and privacy agreements
- map workflows
- define baseline metrics
- complete security review

### Months 4–8 — Limited Launch
- begin with a smaller subset of sites
- train claims and intake staff
- calibrate alert thresholds
- measure alert utility and overrides

### Months 9–14 — Expansion
- add sites only if governance and workflow criteria are met
- audit subgroup performance
- refine integrations
- monitor organizational adoption

### Months 15–18 — Evaluation
- assess preventable issue reduction
- compare operational costs and benefits
- evaluate fairness and patient experience
- review privacy and governance performance
- recommend scale, redesign, or discontinuation

## Cost Drivers
Integration, data engineering, security, staff time, training, model monitoring, legal/governance review, and fairness audits.

The project assumes organizational integration and governance will likely cost more than model training itself.
